About Cynthiagrey

More than decoration.
A complete festive experience.

Cynthiagrey Christmas is redefining the art of Christmas and seasonal décor in Nigeria and across Africa. We combine creativity, craftsmanship, and faith-inspired storytelling to create festive experiences that not only beautify spaces but also connect people to the warmth, love, and joy of the season.

Cynthiagrey Christmas is the Christmas and seasonal décor arm of Cynthiagrey Designs, delivering thoughtful installations, training and creative experiences for homes, businesses and communities.

The brand exists to spread the joy of Christmas through stunning decorations and training that inspire creativity and foster connections.

From corporate offices and restaurants to homes, malls and community spaces, each project is approached with creativity, craftsmanship, balance and attention to detail.
