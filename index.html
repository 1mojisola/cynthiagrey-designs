About Cynthiagrey

Christmas is more than decoration.

Cynthiagrey Christmas is an experiential Christmas and seasonal décor brand under CynthiaGrey Designs, creating festive environments that combine beauty, creativity and meaning.

The brand exists to spread the joy of Christmas through stunning decorations and training while creating experiences that people remember.

From homes and corporate spaces to restaurants, malls and communities, every installation is designed with attention to creativity, balance and the client's vision.

Cynthiagrey Christmas also equips aspiring decorators through training and business-focused masterclasses.
