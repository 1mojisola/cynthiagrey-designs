The Brand

More than décor. A Christmas experience.

Cynthiagrey Christmas is the Christmas and seasonal décor brand under CynthiaGrey Designs, created to redefine the art of festive decoration in Nigeria and across Africa.


We combine creativity, craftsmanship and faith-inspired storytelling to create festive experiences that do more than beautify spaces — they connect people to the warmth, love and joy of the season.


From professional installations to practical décor training, every experience is designed with excellence, creativity and meaning.

What We Do

Designed for beautiful spaces and meaningful experiences.

Whether you are transforming a home, preparing a corporate space for the season or learning how to build a profitable décor business, Cynthiagrey Christmas brings creativity and purpose together.

01

Professional Installations

Bespoke Christmas and seasonal décor installations for corporate offices, restaurants, malls, homes and other spaces that deserve to come alive during the festive season.

02

Training & Masterclasses

Practical workshops and business-focused décor classes designed to equip aspiring and practicing decorators with profitable seasonal decorating skills.

03

Faith-Inspired Storytelling

Themed installations that go beyond aesthetics to communicate stories of hope, light, love and unity through thoughtful Christmas décor.
